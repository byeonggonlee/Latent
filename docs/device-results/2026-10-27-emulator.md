## Found

1. **Sony ARW from older bodies cannot be developed** (decoder, for Codex). `SonyArw.parse` reads white balance only
   from tag `0x7313` in the raw IFD; the ILCE-7M2 (firmware 1.20) and ILCE-7RM2 (1.00) files have none there and fail
   with `Missing white balance`. The viewer still shows their embedded preview; the editor opens empty after a
   short "Could not open the photo." The white balance of these bodies is in the encrypted SR2 private directory,
   which the decoder already decrypts for the colour matrix. Queued in `HANDOFF.md` under *For Codex*.
2. **Editor status bar** (UI, Claude Code): in the light theme the editor's dark workspace keeps dark status-bar
   icons, so the clock and icons are nearly invisible. In the dark theme they are light, as they should be.
3. **Editor after an open failure** (UI, Claude Code): once the snackbar has gone, the editor is a blank picture
   with every control disabled and nothing saying why or what to do.
4. **Selection bar** overflows on a 1080 px-wide screen (known from the S26 check; UI guide task 4).
5. *Feather* at 0 draws its active track from the thumb to the middle, as a two-sided slider would (UI guide task 3).
